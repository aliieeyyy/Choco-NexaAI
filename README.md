# 🍫 ChocoNexa AI
### NLP-Powered Chocolate & Dates Shopping Assistant

**ChocoNexa AI** is an intelligent shopping assistant powered by Natural Language Processing (NLP), designed to simplify the shopping experience for chocolate and dates products.

Instead of searching through traditional product listings, customers can interact with the application using natural language to find products, explore recommendations, and get answers to shopping-related questions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

ChocoNexa AI aims to make online shopping more interactive and convenient through Natural Language Processing.

The system understands customer queries and identifies their shopping requirements to provide relevant product information and recommendations.

It focuses on three major functionalities:

- Intelligent Product Search
- Personalized Product Recommendations
- Customer Query Assistance

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Develop an NLP-based shopping assistant.
- Understand customer queries written in natural language.
- Simplify chocolate and dates product discovery.
- Recommend products based on customer preferences.
- Help customers find products within their budget.
- Improve the overall shopping experience.
